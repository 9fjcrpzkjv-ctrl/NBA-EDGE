Draftstars player analysis · independent model workspace · mobile ready

1. Load your Draftstars player pool

On iPhone, choose Browse → Downloads or On My iPhone → select the .csv file. If this page is inside a preview, open it in Safari first.

A sample player pool is preloaded so you can explore the app. Upload each new slate's CSV to replace it.

2. Projection model

Your CSV currently contains Fantasy Insider projections, not historical game logs. NBA Edge will not pretend those are independent projections. Add the optional historical columns shown below to activate our own model.

Expected columns: PLAYER_NAME or Name, GAME_DATE or Date, MIN, PTS, FGM, FGA, FTM, FTA, FG3M, REB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV. The app calculates Draftstars fantasy points from each box score, then blends last 10 games, season average and recent-vs-season minutes. Player names are matched to your Draftstars pool.

Benchmark mode is active. Independent mode becomes available when historical inputs are present.

4. Generate lineups

Roster template: 2 PG · 2 SG · 2 SF · 2 PF · 1 C. Players with multi-position eligibility can fill either slot.

Generate 20 candidate lineups to compare one paid entry against 19 virtual entries.

5. Results tracker

One $15 paid lineup; virtual lineups are tracked at no cost. Keep a record of every slate to evaluate profitability over time.

Model notes

This is a local browser app. Your CSV is processed on your device and is not sent to a server. Historical logs are imported from your uploaded CSV. It does not fetch live injury news, game logs or starting lineups automatically. Verify late news and contest rules before entering. Lineup scores and projections are estimates, not guarantees of winnings.
