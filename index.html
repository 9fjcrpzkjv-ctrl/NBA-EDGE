7. Ownership and leverage (optional)

Enter estimated ownership percentages from a trusted source, one player per line: Player Name,Ownership%. Example: Nikola Jokic,32.5. This does not change projections. Standard tournament ranking remains active unless you explicitly select leverage ranking below. Missing ownership values are treated neutrally.

Auto estimates are modelled, not live contest ownership. The model distributes approximately 900% total ownership across a nine-player roster pool using projection, salary value, starter status and injury-replacement opportunity. Manual estimates override modelled values.

Model notes

This is a local browser app. Your CSV is processed on your device and is not sent to a server. Historical logs are imported from your uploaded CSV. It does not fetch live injury news, game logs or starting lineups automatically. Verify late news and contest rules before entering. Lineup scores and projections are estimates, not guarantees of winnings.
